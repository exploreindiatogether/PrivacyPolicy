Summary

The short version: the documents you scan, import, or edit in Scanly are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your document content, do not require you to create an account, and do not send document content or extracted (OCR) text to any analytics or advertising service. Cloud backup and multi-device sync are not currently offered.

Scanly does use Google AdMob to show ads (unless you purchase Premium) and Firebase to collect anonymous crash/diagnostic reports and remotely configure app behavior. These services may collect device-level identifiers as described below.

Information We Collect

Category What it includes Leaves your device?
Scanned & imported documents Photos/scans, generated PDFs, page images, OCR text, tags, folders, favorites No — stored locally only
App settings Theme, default filter, App Lock preference and PIN (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), sort order No — stored locally only
Device & advertising identifiers Advertising ID, device model, OS version, IP-based approximate location, ad interaction data Yes — to Google AdMob, only if you are not a Premium subscriber
Diagnostic & crash data Stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, ANR (App Not Responding) reports Yes — to Firebase Crashlytics
Purchase status Whether you own Premium, which plan, purchase token/receipt Yes — handled entirely by Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase

We never see, collect, or have access to the content of your scanned documents or extracted OCR text. They are never transmitted to us, to Firebase, or to any advertising or analytics provider.

Camera & Photo Library

Scanly requests camera access to let you scan physical documents, and photo library access to let you import existing images, documents, or signatures. These permissions are used only at the moment you choose to scan or import something — the app does not access your camera or photo library in the background, and captured images are saved only to your device's local app storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free (non-Premium) use of Scanly is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and other device information to serve and measure ads, and may use this information consistent with Google's Partner Policy. Ads are never shown inside the active scanning/camera flow.

Purchasing Premium removes all ads and stops this data sharing with AdMob going forward.

Diagnostics & Crash Reports

Scanly uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically report app crashes and ANRs (App Not Responding events), and Firebase Remote Config to adjust app behavior (such as ad frequency) without requiring an app update. These reports include technical information (device model, OS version, app version, stack traces) but never your document content, filenames, or OCR text.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Premium purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple's In-App Purchase (iOS). Scanly never receives or stores your payment card details — we only receive confirmation of what you purchased and its expiry, which we store locally to unlock Premium features on your device.

How Your Data Is Stored

All documents, folders, tags, favorites, OCR text, and settings are stored locally in Scanly's app-private storage on your device. Cloud backup and multi-device sync are not implemented in the current version of the app. This means:

  • Your documents are only ever on the device(s) where you scanned or imported them.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, permanently deletes your documents.
  • We cannot recover your documents for you, since we never had a copy.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited, non-document data only with:

  • Google AdMob — device/advertising identifiers, for ad serving (non-Premium users only).
  • Google Firebase — crash/diagnostic data and remote configuration values.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — purchase and subscription processing.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Scanly, our users, or the public.

Third-Party Services

For details on how our service providers handle data, see their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (covers AdMob and Firebase)
  • Google Play Billing
  • Apple Privacy Policy (covers In-App Purchase on iOS)

Your Choices & Controls

  • App Lock: Protect the app with a PIN or biometric lock (Settings → App Lock).
  • Remove ads: Purchase Premium to stop ad serving and related data sharing with AdMob.
  • Delete documents: Delete any document at any time; deleted items are held in "Recently Deleted" for 30 days before permanent removal, or you can permanently delete immediately.
  • Advertising ID: Reset or limit ad tracking for your device in your OS's system settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Uninstall: Removes all locally stored documents and settings from your device.

Data Retention & Deletion

Documents and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Crash/diagnostic data sent to Firebase Crashlytics is retained according to Firebase's data retention practices.

Children's Privacy

Scanly is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.

Security

Your documents are stored in the app's private, sandboxed storage, which the operating system prevents other apps from accessing without permission. App Lock PINs are stored as salted cryptographic hashes, never in plain text. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to a local-first app to protect your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes — for example, if cloud backup/sync is introduced in the future. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
