1Overview

PDF Reader ("the App", "we", "us") is a mobile application for viewing, annotating, organizing, and managing PDF documents that already exist on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and the third-party services it uses collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: your documents never leave your device. All PDF viewing, annotation, merging, splitting, rotating, reordering, and signing happens locally, on-device. We do not operate a server that stores, scans, or has access to your files. The only data that leaves your device is what's described in this policy — mainly technical/diagnostic data from the third-party services listed below, and purchase data handled directly by Google Play / the App Store.

2Information we collect

Information you provide directly

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information (name, email, phone number) to use it. We do not operate any backend server that stores personal profile information about you.

Information collected automatically

Like most apps that use ads, crash reporting, and in-app purchases, the App relies on third-party SDKs that automatically collect certain technical information. This can include:

  • Device information (model, manufacturer, OS version, locale, screen size)
  • App diagnostics (crash logs, stack traces, app version, session duration)
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID / Apple's IDFA, where available and permitted)
  • Approximate location (country/region only, derived from IP address, used for ad compliance and localization — never precise GPS location)
  • In-app purchase/subscription status (which plan is active, purchase timestamps) — processed by Google Play / Apple, not by us

We do not use this information to build an advertising profile of you ourselves — it is collected and processed by the third-party providers described in the next section, under their own privacy policies.

3Your documents & local data

The following stays only on your device and is never uploaded by the App:

  • The content of any PDF you open, import, annotate, merge, split, rotate, reorder, or sign
  • Any highlight, underline, strikethrough, sticky note, or freehand annotation you add
  • Any signature you draw or import from your photo library
  • Your recent-documents list, favorites, and reading position, stored locally in the app's on-device preferences storage
  • The folder you choose to browse in the "Storage" tab (the App only accesses the specific folder you select via the system file picker, not your entire device storage)

If you use the Share or Print features, the App hands the file to the operating system's native share/print sheet — at that point, where the file goes is controlled by you and by the app or printer you choose, not by us.

4Third-party services

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

ServicePurposeProvider's policy
Firebase Crashlytics Collects crash reports and error logs so we can fix bugs. Includes device model, OS version, app version, and stack traces — not the content of your documents. Firebase Privacy
Firebase Remote Config Lets us remotely tune app behavior (e.g. how often ads are shown) without an app update. Involves a Firebase-generated installation identifier, not personal data. Firebase Privacy

5App permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, only for the stated purpose:

  • Storage / file access — to let you pick a PDF to open, or a folder to browse, via the system file picker. The App does not request broad "all files" access; you choose exactly what it can see.
  • Photo library — only used if you choose to import an existing signature image instead of drawing one.
  • Internet — required for ads, Remote Config, crash reporting, and processing purchases.
  • In-app billing — required to offer the optional Premium subscription.

6Advertising & personalization

Free (non-Premium) use of the App is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Depending on your device settings and region, these ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier and general interests, or non-personalized. You can control ad personalization at the OS level:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalized Ads.

Purchasing Premium removes all ads from the App entirely.

7Subscriptions & purchases

The App offers an optional Premium unlock (lifetime, monthly, or yearly) that removes ads and unlocks the PDF Tools (merge, split, rotate, reorder, sign, print). All purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store using the payment method on file with your Google/Apple account. We only receive confirmation of which plan you purchased and its status (active/ expired) — never your card or account details.

8Data retention & deletion

Your documents, annotations, and library metadata live in local app storage on your device and are deleted automatically if you uninstall the App, or manually via Profile → Clear Reading History inside the App. Diagnostic data held by Firebase Crashlytics is retained per Google's own retention schedule (typically up to 90 days) and is not linked to an identifiable person.

9Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

10Security

Because your documents and library data stay on your device rather than on a server we control, the main safeguard is your own device's security (passcode, encryption, OS updates). Data transmitted to the third-party services above (crash reports, ad requests, purchase receipts) is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

11Your choices & rights

  • Access/delete local data: use Profile → Clear Reading History in the App, or uninstall the App, to remove all locally stored library data.
  • Ad personalization: controlled via your device's OS settings (see "Advertising & personalization" above).
  • Restore purchases: available from Profile → Restore Purchases.
  • Regional privacy rights (e.g. GDPR, CCPA/CPRA): depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you by our third-party providers. Since we don't hold a personal profile ourselves, requests about data collected by Firebase or AdMob are best directed to Google's own privacy controls, and you're welcome to contact us for anything App-specific.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App's features change. We'll update the "Effective date" above when we do. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
