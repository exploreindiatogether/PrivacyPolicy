The basics

Who we are

(the "App") is a collection of casual puzzle and arcade games for Android, published by ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By using the App you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the App.

Loot check

What we collect

The App only asks for Internet and network-state access. It never asks for:

NameEmailPhone ContactsPhotosGPS location CameraMicrophone

Sent to our servers

  • Anonymous player ID. On first launch the App creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account. This random ID isn't linked to your name or email.
  • Game data. Your coin balance, wallet transactions (coins earned, spent or bought), daily reward claims and game progress such as levels completed and scores.
  • Device record. A one-way hash (SHA-256) of your device's Android ID, your device model (for example "Pixel 8") and the App version. We store only the hash, never the raw Android ID, and use it to restore your coins and progress if you reinstall on the same device.
  • Purchase records. The product you bought, the Google Play order ID, a hash of the purchase token and its status (for example completed or refunded). We never receive your card or payment details.

Collected by Google services in the App

  • Device and app info such as device model, Android version, language, screen size, App version and an app instance ID.
  • Advertising ID, Google's resettable advertising identifier, used by AdMob and, where allowed, Firebase Analytics.
  • Usage info such as when you open the App, screens viewed, session length, and which ads were shown or tapped.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general region (country or city), for example to decide whether the consent form is needed.
  • Integrity signals that confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the App on a genuine device (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity).

Kept only on your device

Game saves, settings, a local copy of your progress and coins, and downloaded game files live in the App's private storage. They're deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Game mechanics

How we use information

  • To run the games and keep your coins, purchases and progress in sync and safe from loss.
  • To restore your progress after you reinstall the App on the same device.
  • To verify and deliver in-app purchases, and to handle refunds.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards.
  • To understand how the App is used, fix crashes and bugs, and improve the games.
  • To stop fraud, cheating and abuse, such as fake purchases or tampered copies of the App.
  • To change App settings remotely without an update (Firebase Remote Config).
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax and accounting rules for purchases.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we rely on performance of a contract to provide the games, your game data and purchases; legitimate interests for analytics, security and fraud prevention; your consent for personalized ads and the device storage they need; and legal obligation where the law requires us to keep records.

Ads and your consent

The App is free and supported by ads. Ads can be personalized (based on your interests, using your advertising ID) or non-personalized (based on general context like the App itself and your approximate region). Here's how you stay in control:

  1. First launch. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it, Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested. Accept, refuse, or pick specific purposes.
  2. Change your mind anytime. Open the App's More tab and tap Privacy & ad choices (shown where consent applies).
  3. Everywhere. Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).
We don't sell your personal information for money. Under some US state laws, sharing data with ad partners for personalized ads may count as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out using the steps above.

The shop

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Your payment details are handled only by Google and are never shared with us. Google Play sends us a purchase token and order details so we can confirm the purchase and add the coins or items to your account. Purchases are tied to your anonymous player ID through a hashed value, so we can restore them and handle refunds.

When we share information

Apart from the services listed in Level 4, we share information only:

  • when required by law, a court order or a valid request from a public authority;
  • to protect the rights, safety or property of our players, ourselves or others, including to investigate fraud;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy keeps applying to your data.

Save slots

How long we keep data

  • Game data and device record: kept while you use the App so your progress can be restored. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see Level 11).
  • Purchase records: kept as long as needed for refunds, fraud prevention and legal, tax and accounting requirements, even if you ask us to delete your other data.
  • Analytics and ads data: kept by Google under its own retention settings. Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.
  • Data on your device: kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Shields up

Security

Everything sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database can only be reached by our server code, never directly by the App. Every request is checked against your anonymous sign-in and Firebase App Check. We store hashes instead of raw device IDs and purchase tokens.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

International transfers

Our servers run on Google Cloud / Firebase in the United States (region ). Google may also process data in other countries. When data leaves the EEA, UK or Switzerland, Google relies on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Side quest: delete your data

You play without a login, so we need a few clues to find your data:

  1. Open the App's More tab and note your Player ID (for example #A1B2C3).
  2. Email with the subject "Delete my data", including your Player ID, device model and roughly when you installed the App.
  3. We'll delete your game data and device record within 30 days and confirm by email.

Purchase records we must keep by law stay as described in Level 8. Uninstalling the App removes everything stored on your device.
