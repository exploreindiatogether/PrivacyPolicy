Section 3

On-Device AI & Facial Landmarks Processing

Beauty Camera includes smart beauty features such as facial contouring, eye enlargement, smile enhancement, skin smoothing, acne spot healing, lipstick tinting, and subject cutouts. We want to be 100% transparent about how this technology works:

How Facial Features are Processed

  • Local Detection Only: We utilize Google ML Kit (Face Detection, Pose Detection, and Selfie Segmentation libraries) which execute entirely on your device's local neural engine / CPU / GPU.
  • No Biometric Identification: We do not extract or generate biometric templates, facial recognition profiles, or any information that can identify a specific person. The algorithms only detect generic geometric landmarks (e.g. coordinates of eye contours, lips, and face boundaries) to guide shader transformations.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Lifecycle: Landmark coordinates exist solely in temporary RAM for the fractions of a second needed to render your live preview or process your edited photo. When the frame is rendered or the photo is exported, these coordinates are immediately released from memory.
  • No Cloud AI: We do not send your facial images, video frames, or landmark points to cloud services, third-party AI APIs, or remote databases.
Summary on Facial Data

We do not collect facial data. We do not store facial data. We do not share facial data. All facial processing is local, transient, and private to your device.

Section 4

Third-Party Services & Libraries

To maintain app performance, monitor crashes, and support free access through advertising, Beauty Camera integrates select third-party SDKs provided by Google. These services may collect certain device-level information as described below:

1. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, app open, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and process:

  • IP address (for coarse geographic location to serve relevant ads).
  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA).
  • App interaction data and ad diagnostic metrics (ad impressions, clicks).

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: Google Advertising & Privacy.

2. Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to dynamically adjust app settings (such as enabling/disabling ads and configuring ad display frequencies without requiring an app store update). Remote Config fetches non-personal configuration key-values.

3. Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics

To ensure stability across thousands of Android and iOS device models, we use Firebase Crashlytics. When a rare crash occurs, Crashlytics collects anonymous stack traces, device hardware specs, operating system versions, and crash timestamps. No photos, camera inputs, or user identifiers are included in crash logs.

Review Firebase's data privacy documentation: Firebase Privacy and Security.

Section 5

Advertising Policy & Opt-Out Choices

We display advertisements to keep Beauty Camera free for all users. You have full control over whether ads shown to you are personalized based on your device interests:

How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

  • On Android: Open your device Settings > Google > Ads > Tap Delete advertising ID or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Turn off tracking permissions for apps.

Opting out does not reduce the number of ads shown, but ensures ads will be non-personalized and not based on previous browsing or app activity.

Section 6

Data Storage, Retention & Security

Because Beauty Camera does not maintain user accounts or cloud storage for photos:

  • Local Gallery Retention: Photos and videos saved by you remain in your device's photo gallery under your sole control. You can delete them at any time using your standard Photos or Gallery app.
  • App Preferences: Settings like chosen aspect ratios, timer preferences, grid options, and beauty presets are stored locally via Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults. They are erased if you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • Security Measures: We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS) for all network communications made by third-party diagnostic and advertising libraries.
