1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [Developer/Company Legal Name] ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Hindu Calendar (Panchang) mobile application (the "App"), available for Android and iOS.

The App provides Hindu Panchang (calendar) details, festival and Vrat (fasting) calendars, Muhurat timings, Kundli, Horoscope, religious content (mantras, aartis, chalisas, kathas), sacred temple information, and related reminders and notifications.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only what is needed to calculate accurate Panchang data and operate the App's features.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Calculate and display Panchang, Festival, Vrat, Muhurat, Kundli, and Horoscope data for your selected location(s).
  • Save your Favorites, custom Reminders, and Saved Locations across app sessions.
  • Send reminder notifications you have explicitly opted into.
  • Verify and grant Premium / "Remove Ads" purchases.
  • Understand feature usage through aggregated, anonymized analytics.
  • Serve advertising to free-tier users, and measure ad performance.
  • Maintain app security, including Firebase App Check verification that requests genuinely originate from this App.

We do not use your data to build an advertising profile of you outside of the App, and we do not sell your personal information.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Location permission — you can deny or revoke location access in your device settings at any time; you can still manually search/select a city under Saved Locations.
  • Notifications — enable or disable each reminder category (festival, vrat, muhurat, daily Panchang) from within the App, or disable all notifications from your device settings.
  • Advertising — you can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in your device's OS settings, or remove ads entirely via the "Remove Ads"/Premium purchase.
  • Data deletion — you can request deletion of the data associated with your anonymous account by contacting us (see Section 12). Uninstalling the App removes all data stored locally on your device.
  • Access/correction — since we do not collect a name or email, most of your data (Favorites, Reminders, Saved Locations) is directly visible and editable from within the App itself.

7. Data Retention

We retain data associated with your anonymous account for as long as you use the App, or until you request deletion. Cached Panchang/Festival/Vrat calendar data is retained server-side to avoid recomputing the same astronomical data repeatedly, and is periodically cleaned up. Analytics data is retained per Google's standard Firebase Analytics retention settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including Firebase App Check to verify requests originate from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App, and Firestore security rules that restrict each user's data to that user alone. Server-side purchase verification (against the Google Play Developer API / Apple's receipt verification service) protects against fraudulent Premium activation. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
