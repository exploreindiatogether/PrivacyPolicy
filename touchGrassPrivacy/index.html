At a glance

What we see

  • Which app opened, and when (for example, "Instagram opened at 11:42"). This stays on your device.
  • Purchase details, so we can unlock Pro
  • Basic, anonymous paywall events

What we never see

  • Your messages, feeds, photos or anything inside other apps
  • Photos you take for the photo challenge (checked on your device, then thrown away)
  • Your name, email address, contacts or location

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads.

Who we are

Touch Grass (package com.tejaslabs.touchgrass) is made by Tejas Labs ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the Touch Grass Android app ("the app") handles, why it handles it, and the choices you have.

Data that stays on your device

The app works by itself on your phone. The following is stored only in the app's private storage and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • App usage statistics: which apps you use and for how long. We read these through Android's Usage Access to show your screen time and to notice when you open an app you've chosen to guard.
  • Your settings: the apps you guard, the challenges you choose, your language and other preferences.
  • Block history and stats: when a block screen appeared, which challenge you completed, and your streaks.
  • Step counts: counted during a walking challenge only, using your phone's step sensor.
  • Photo challenge images: analysed in your phone's memory by a machine-learning model built into the app. They are never saved to your gallery and never uploaded.

If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include the app's local data in your personal Google backup. Google handles that backup under its own policies. We cannot see or access it.

Data we collect

We collect a small amount of data, for these purposes only:

1. Anonymous app identifier

When you first open the app, it signs in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID that is not linked to your name, email or Google account. We use it only to tie your Pro purchase to your installation.

2. Purchase information

If you buy Touch Grass Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We never receive your card or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is real, the app sends the Google Play purchase token and product ID to our server. The server checks them with Google Play and stores:

  • your anonymous ID, the product ID and the purchase token
  • whether the purchase is valid, its expiry date and whether it auto-renews
  • the verification response from Google Play and the time it was checked

3. Analytics (paywall events)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how people use the upgrade screen. We record events such as "paywall viewed", "checkout started", "purchase completed" and "purchase check failed", along with the product and billing period, and whether you have Pro. Firebase may also collect standard device information, such as device model, OS version, app version, country (from your IP address) and an app-instance ID.

We do not send your app usage, guarded apps, stats, photos or steps to analytics.

4. Remote configuration

The app downloads settings, such as the list of available Pro plans, from Firebase Remote Config. This request includes basic technical information, such as an app-instance ID, which Firebase needs to deliver the settings.

5. Things you choose to send us

  • Feedback: if you use "Help & feedback", your email app opens with your message. We receive whatever you send, including your email address.
  • Sharing stats: if you share a stats card, an image is created on your device and passed to the app you pick. We don't receive it.

Third-party services

These services process data for us under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Billing: payments and subscriptions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Authentication, Analytics, Remote Config, Cloud Functions, Firestore) by Google. Privacy and Security in Firebase

How we share data

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share data only:

  • with the service providers listed above, so the app can work
  • when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public
  • with a new owner if Tejas Labs or the app is sold or merged, under the same protections described here

Data retention

  • On-device data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records are kept while your purchase or subscription is active, and afterwards for as long as we reasonably need them for accounting, refunds, fraud prevention or legal reasons.
  • Analytics data is kept under Firebase's retention settings, which are no longer than 14 months.
  • Feedback emails are kept for as long as we need them to help you.

Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Our purchase database is locked so that apps cannot read or write to it directly. Only our verification server can. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off any permission in Android Settings at any time.
  • Reset your advertising and app-instance identifiers in Android's Google settings.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (purchases), our legitimate interests (keeping the app working and improving it), and your consent (where required). To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Deleting your data

Touch Grass has no traditional account. To delete your data:

  1. On your device: uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Touch Grass → Storage → Clear data.
  2. On our servers: email support@touchgrass.app with the subject "Data deletion request". If you can, include your Google Play order ID (it starts with GPA.) so we can find your purchase record. We will delete your anonymous ID and purchase records within 30 days, except for anything we must keep by law, such as transaction records for tax purposes.

Deleting your data does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.

Children

Touch Grass is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app or on our Play Store listing.
